# Community standards

Be precise, constructive, and respectful. Critique decisions and code, not people. Respect privacy, consent, accessibility needs, and the security boundaries of other contributors' systems.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private user data, and weaponizing security reports are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact a maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue.
